## Scope corrections and deviations

- PS allows APIs; the no-runtime-model policy is an adopted brief preference plus repair of an unsafe provenance claim.
- The general recall claim is narrowed to the one-hot-value fixture. Formula 1/m is exact for U=I; RoPE correction is measured. A tie is not strict success.
- Welch forces overlap, not necessarily class argmax failure. The lift is corpus-calibrated teaching code, not BDH's learned encoder or a held-out theorem validation. Its displayed capacity is seed/class/corpus dependent.
- Claude's exploratory lift numbers were not pasted. The generated results differ: at seed 17, N=1024 and 5% activity the 128-pair sweep is right-censored. Lower activity can yield zero codes; Welch is then displayed as not applicable.
- The broader causal claim about production models' recall weakness is omitted. Architecture cards are published descriptions, not a diagnosis or quality ranking.
- Byte-identical refers to held-out query inputs in Table 3, not identical context/compute interventions. The 3/24 correct-dimensions statistic belongs to a separate ordering diagnostic, not Table 3's short-context row.
- Color-swap acquisition weakness confounds its composition failure. Combined identifier/batch intervention is not a clean factorial experiment.
- The trip scenario is an original simplified three-fact assay, not a reproduced BDH-CQ trip case.
- Write-rule parallel results use an independent direct affine-product expansion. Optimized compact WY/UT and KDA DPLR kernels remain cited literature, not implemented kernels.
- The hand-authored synthesis SVG labels each directed relation and its source markers. Reading notes supply equivalent prose and focusable citations; no implementation ancestry or external model/checkpoint replay is implied.
